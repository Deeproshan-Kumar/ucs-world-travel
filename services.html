GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our travel, booking, and wallet services.

01 How can I book flights, buses, or cars with UCS?
You can easily book online through our dedicated flights, buses, and cars booking pages, or reach out directly to our customer care team via phone or email for custom bookings and assistance.
02 How does the UCS Wallet recharge process work?
You can deposit money directly into our verified bank accounts listed on the Bank Information page. Submit your deposit entry in your account dashboard, and once verified, your wallet balance will be credited for fast, 1-click booking checkout.
03 What is the cancellation and refund policy?
Cancellations are processed according to the operator or airline guidelines. Once approved, refunds are credited back to your original payment method or UCS Wallet promptly in accordance with our Refund Policy.
04 Can I customize a holiday tour itinerary for my family?
Yes, absolutely! We specialize in tailor-made holiday packages. Contact our tour experts with your preferred destination, dates, and budget, and we will craft a personalized itinerary including hotels, transport, and sightseeing.
05 Who do I contact in case of an emergency while travelling?
Our 24/7 customer care numbers (+91 8853240990, +91 8840622478) are operational round the clock. You can call us anytime for urgent travel re-routing, vehicle assistance, or trip queries.
